The Challenge

A Continuity Plan in a Binder
Is Not Continuity.

Plans that were written once and never tested, single points of failure no one mapped, recovery objectives that are aspirational rather than agreed, and no crisis playbook for the day it actually matters. For GCCs and IT operations, the parent enterprise and its clients now demand evidence of resilience — not assurances. We build continuity that holds up under scrutiny and under pressure.

What We Do

Five Disciplines.
One Resilient Operation.

Engage a focused assessment or an end-to-end continuity programme. Each discipline is scoped to the realities of GCC and IT delivery — distributed teams, deep technology dependencies, and demanding parent and client expectations.

01

Business Impact & Risk Assessment

Knowing What Can't Stop

A Business Impact Analysis that maps critical processes, dependencies, and tolerable downtime — paired with a threat assessment covering the risks that actually face captive centres and IT operations. The evidence base for every decision that follows.

02

Continuity Strategy

Designing for Resilience

Agreed RTO and RPO targets, site and workforce strategy, and the redundancy design that keeps delivery running when a site, a link, or a team is lost — balanced against cost so resilience is proportionate, not gold-plated.

RTO / RPOSite StrategyWorkforce ResilienceRedundancy
03

IT Disaster Recovery

Technology That Recovers

DR architecture, backup and failover design, and tested recovery runbooks — so systems come back in the time the business agreed to, not the time IT hopes for. The technical core of continuity for an IT-led operation.

DR ArchitectureBackup & FailoverRunbooksRecovery Testing

Crisis Management & Response

Who Decides, and How

An incident command structure, communication protocols, and escalation paths so that when disruption hits, the response is rehearsed and clear — not improvised in a panic. Roles, decision rights, and message templates defined in advance.

Engagement Approach

Diagnose. Design.
Deploy. Sustain.

Evidence builds conviction before any recommendation. Capability is embedded before exit — so results compound rather than regress.

01

Diagnose

A quantified, evidence-led baseline of the current state — before any solution is framed. We start from facts, not assumptions.

02

Design

Target-state design with the operating model, sequencing, and business case attached — so the path forward is concrete, not aspirational.

03

Deploy

Hands-on delivery alongside your teams — not deck-and-leave. We stay embedded until traction is real and visible.

04

Sustain

Operating rhythm, governance, and capability transfer designed so your team owns it — and no longer needs us.
